## 3. Policy Drafting & Technical Grounding

### 3.1 Terms & Conditions (`/terms`)
- **Discovery vs. Booking:** Explicitly establishes that the Platform operates as an informational talent showcase directory. It does **not** provide online booking checkouts, escrow accounts, automated contracts of hire, payment processing, or cancellation dispute arbitration.
- **Verification vs. Self-Publishing:** Clearly distinguishes artist submission and administrative review from the administrative "✓ Verified" badge.
- **Intellectual Property:** Artists retain 100% copyright in their uploaded portfolios, granting The Artist Network a limited, royalty-free, non-exclusive license solely to host and display the portfolio for profile discovery.
- **Copyright Takedown:** Formulates an actionable notice-and-takedown protocol under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
- **Liability Caps & Jurisdiction:** Intermediary protection under Section 79 of the IT Act, 2000, governed by Indian law.

### 3.2 Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)
- **Strict Public vs. Private Separation:**
  - *Publicly Accessible:* Stage name, category, genres, city/state, bio, indicative starting rates, portfolio items, YouTube embeds, Instagram URLs, and technical riders.
  - *Strictly Confidential & Private:* Legal full name (`public.profiles.full_name`), account emails, password hashes, moderation rejection notes, business contact details, and **artist private calendar records** (`client_name`, `client_phone`, `client_email`, `agreed_fee`, `deposit_paid`, and `private_notes`).
- **Cookie & Storage Audit:**
  - `sb-*-auth-token`: Strictly necessary first-party session cookies generated by Supabase Auth (`@supabase/ssr`).
  - `localStorage (tan_auth_user)`: Non-sensitive UI state cache to prevent hydration flicker.
  - `YouTube Embeds (iframe)`: Third-party video player embeds that operate under Google/YouTube's privacy policies.
  - *Zero Ad Trackers:* No Meta Pixel, Google Ads, or behavioral tracking scripts installed.
- **Governing Law:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, 2023) and IT (SPDI) Rules, 2011.

## 4. India-Specific Legal Framework Assessment

### 4.1 Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, 2023)
- **Status in India:** Passed by Parliament and enacted in August 2023. Phased implementation is currently in progress pending notification of the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules and establishment of the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).
- **Applicability:** As a Data Fiduciary processing digital personal data of Indian residents, the platform must adhere to purpose limitation, reasonable security safeguards, and respect data principal rights (access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal).
- **Section 9 Compliance (Children):** The platform explicitly restricts eligibility to individuals aged 18 and older. No tracking or targeted behavioral ads are directed at minors.

### 4.2 Information Technology Act, 2000 & Intermediary Guidelines (2021)
- **Safe Harbour under Section 79:** To maintain intermediary protection from liability for third-party user content (artist stage names, bios, photos, YouTube links), the platform has:
  1. Published Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines.
  2. Defined clear prohibitions against illegal, defamatory, obscene, infringing, or impersonating content.
  3. Established a statutory Grievance Officer channel and notice-and-takedown mechanism.
  4. Preserved moderation audit trails to demonstrate administrative due diligence.

### 4.3 Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
- **Assessment:** Because The Artist Network currently operates as a free talent discovery directory with **zero** transactional checkouts, fee commissions, or payment gateways, it is not currently functioning as a transactional e-commerce entity. Once Phase 5 (online booking/payments) is implemented, the platform must incorporate mandatory seller disclosures, invoice transparency, and formal refund policies.

4. **Phase 5 Gate:** Direct booking and client enquiry workflows are not yet implemented. Platform messaging must consistently reflect this until Phase 5 is authorized and built.
